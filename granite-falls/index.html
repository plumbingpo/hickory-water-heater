Keeping Your Granite Falls Water Heater Running Longer

A little routine care makes all the difference between a water heater that lasts and one that fails early. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a yearly flush, an anode rod check every three to five years, a quick relief valve test once a year, a thermostat set near 120 degrees, and yearly descaling for any tankless unit on hard water, so small issues get caught early.

How old is your water heater? The rating plate on the tank usually encodes the month and year it was built, and that date says a lot about what comes next. Most tank models make it to 8 to 12 years, heat pump units 10 to 15, and regularly descaled tankless units about 20. If your heater is beyond that range and also shows discolored water, rumbling, or dampness under the tank, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency replacement after a leak.

Small problems rarely stay small when they're ignored. Consider a spent anode rod: swapping it out is a short, affordable repair, but once it's gone, the water begins corroding the tank steel, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and reach out at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and prevents a surprise cleanup.

Hot Water Headaches Granite Falls Homeowners Face

Every type of water heater has its own weak points, and we work on all of them around Granite Falls. Gas tank heaters usually give trouble through thermocouples and flame sensors, dirty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric tanks burn out elements and thermostats, especially when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless heaters show error codes for scale buildup, ignition faults, and flow sensor trouble, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Hybrid heat pump units depend on a clean filter and a free-flowing condensate line, and they don't do well in small, closed-in spaces. Our technicians work on all four every week and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.
