Restaurant Services

Restaurant and Food Service Water Heaters

Few businesses work a water heater harder than a restaurant. Prep sinks, pre-rinse sprayers, and dish cycles all draw from the same tank, a lunch rush can empty a small heater in minutes, and the dish machine needs a booster to reach its sanitizing temperature. Hickory Water Heater services, sizes, and repairs the tanks, tankless units, and booster heaters that keep the kitchen and restrooms supplied, and we schedule service around your hours.

Many restaurant inspection problems trace back to water temperature. If the main heater can't keep up, handwashing sinks go lukewarm, the three-compartment sink falls below temperature, and the dish machine can't hit its final rinse during the rush. We verify temperatures at the tank, the booster, and the fixtures and document every visit in written reports your inspector and landlord can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing after close when needed, keeping downtime short, and keeping part of the system online where possible.

Safety

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to provide safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can lead to carbon monoxide hazards from bad venting, scalding at the tap, bacterial growth in tanks kept too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Hickory Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas piping sizing, relief valve discharge piping, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where local code calls for it, and permits and inspections. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. Every account gets a documentation binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test results, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms, is put on a more frequent service schedule.
